    Where to pray and eat

    Certification is not the same everywhere, so every restaurant carries one of three labels. Check the certificate on the wall if it matters to you. Places marked Confirm before visiting have conflicting or older sources.

    Fully halal Entire menu halal or a certified kitchen
    Certified halal menu Certified dishes, but the venue may serve alcohol or non-halal food
    Muslim-friendly Halal meat or pork-free, certification not confirmed

    Essentials

    Ask, read the label, find the stamp

    Show these to staff, check convenience-store labels, and know which halal certifiers you will see on restaurant walls in Japan.

    Show-to-staff phrases

    Tap copy, then show the Japanese on your screen.

    I am Muslim. I cannot eat pork or drink alcohol.

    私はムスリムです。豚肉とお酒は口にできません。

    Watashi wa Musurimu desu. Butaniku to osake wa kuchi ni dekimasen.

    Does this contain pork, lard, or gelatin?

    これには豚肉、ラード、ゼラチンが入っていますか？

    Kore ni wa butaniku, rādo, zerachin ga haitte imasu ka?

    Is mirin, cooking sake or alcohol used in the sauce or soup?

    タレやスープに、みりん・料理酒・お酒は使われていますか？

    Tare ya sūpu ni, mirin, ryōrishu, osake wa tsukawarete imasu ka?

    Is this meat halal-certified? May I see the certificate?

    このお肉はハラール認証のものですか？認証書を見せていただけますか？

    Kono oniku wa harāru ninshō no mono desu ka? Ninshōsho o misete itadakemasu ka?

    Is there a quiet place where I can pray for about 10 minutes?

    10分ほどお祈りできる静かな場所はありますか？

    Juppun hodo oinori dekiru shizuka na basho wa arimasu ka?

    Ingredient label decoder

    Ingredients are listed under 原材料名 (genzairyō-mei) on the back of packs.

    豚肉・豚・ポーク
    butaniku · buta · pōku — pork
    Avoid
    ラード・豚脂
    rādo · tonshi — lard, pork fat
    Avoid
    ベーコン・ハム・ポークエキス
    bēkon · hamu · pōku ekisu — bacon, ham, pork extract
    Avoid
    洋酒・酒精・アルコール
    yōshu · shusei · arukōru — liquor, ethanol
    Avoid
    みりん・料理酒
    mirin · ryōrishu — sweet rice wine, cooking sake
    Scholars differ
    ゼラチン・コラーゲン
    zerachin · korāgen — gelatin, collagen (often porcine)
    Check source
    乳化剤・ショートニング
    nyūkazai · shōtoningu — emulsifier, shortening
    Check source
    牛肉・鶏肉・チキンエキス
    gyūniku · toriniku — beef, chicken (not halal-slaughtered unless stated)
    Needs halal mark
    植物油脂・大豆由来
    shokubutsu yushi · daizu yurai — vegetable fat, soy-derived
    Plant-based
    寒天・ペクチン
    kanten · pekuchin — agar, pectin
