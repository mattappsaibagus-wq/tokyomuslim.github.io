    The directory · checked

    Where to pray, eat and shop

    Halal certification varies in Japan, so every restaurant carries one of three labels. If it matters to you, ask to see the certificate. Places marked Confirm before visiting have conflicting or older sources.

    Fully halalThe entire menu is halal, or the kitchen is certified.
    Certified halal menuCertified dishes or courses. The venue may also serve alcohol or non-halal food.
    Muslim-friendlyHalal meat or pork-free, but certification is not confirmed.

    Essentials for the journey

    Ask, read the label, find the stamp

    Phrases to show staff, a decoder for convenience-store labels, and the halal certifiers you will see on restaurant walls in Japan.

    Show-to-staff phrases

    Tap copy, then show the Japanese on your screen.

    I am Muslim

    私はムスリムです。豚肉とお酒は口にできません。

    I cannot eat pork or drink alcohol.

    Pork, lard, gelatin

    これには豚肉、ラード、ゼラチンが入っていますか？

    Does this contain pork, lard or gelatin?

    Alcohol in cooking

    タレやスープに、みりん・料理酒・お酒は使われていますか？

    Is mirin, cooking sake or alcohol used in the sauce or soup?

    Halal certificate

    このお肉はハラール認証のものですか？認証書を見せていただけますか？

    Is this meat halal-certified? May I see the certificate?

    A place to pray

    10分ほどお祈りできる静かな場所はありますか？

    Is there a quiet place where I can pray for about 10 minutes?

    Ingredient label decoder

    Ingredients are listed under 原材料名 (genzairyō-mei) on the back of packs.

    豚肉・豚・ポーク
    butaniku · buta · pōku — pork
    Avoid
    ラード・豚脂
    rādo · tonshi — lard, pork fat
    Avoid
    ベーコン・ハム・ポークエキス
    bēkon · hamu · pōku ekisu — bacon, ham, pork extract
    Avoid
    洋酒・酒精・アルコール
    yōshu · shusei · arukōru — liquor, ethanol
    Avoid
    みりん・料理酒
    mirin · ryōrishu — sweet rice wine, cooking sake
    Scholars differ
    ゼラチン・コラーゲン
    zerachin · korāgen — gelatin, collagen (often porcine)
    Check source
    乳化剤・ショートニング
    nyūkazai · shōtoningu — emulsifier, shortening
    Check source
    牛肉・鶏肉・チキンエキス
    gyūniku · toriniku — beef, chicken (not halal-slaughtered unless stated)
    Needs halal mark
    植物油脂・大豆由来
    shokubutsu yushi · daizu yurai — vegetable fat, soy-derived
    Plant-based
    寒天・ペクチン
    kanten · pekuchin — agar, pectin

    Certifiers you will see in Japan

    Look for these names on the certificate by the entrance or the register.

    Japan Halal FoundationLinked to Masjid Assalam, Okachimachi. Common in Ueno and Asakusa.
    Japan Islamic TrustOtsuka Mosque's organisation. Certifies restaurants and products.
    Nippon Asia Halal AssociationNAHA, a Tokyo-based certifier for restaurants and food makers.
    Japan Halal AssociationJHA, Osaka-based. Recognised by JAKIM.
    Malaysia Halal CorporationCertifies some wagyu courses in Tokyo.
    Japan Muslim AssociationJapan's oldest Muslim organisation, founded 1952.
